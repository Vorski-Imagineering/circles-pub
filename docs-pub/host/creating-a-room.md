# Creating a room

A room is a permanent place, not a scheduled meeting. You make it once and reuse
the same link every time you gather.

Go to **Rooms** in the admin area and choose **Add room**.

## The settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Room name** | Becomes part of the URL, so choose it carefully — letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores only. |
| **Room description** | A private note to yourself about what this room is for. Participants never see it. |
| **Host** | Who owns the room. Admins can assign this to another host; as a host you are the default. |
| **Speaker mode** | Which of the three layouts the circle uses. |
| **Goodbye URL** | Where people are sent when you end the circle. Optional. |
| **Scene** | The background and centre media. See [Choosing a scene](choosing-a-scene.md). |

## Speaker mode

Three options, described in full from the participant's side in
[Speaking and the centre](../circle/speaking-and-the-centre.md):

- **Speaker stays centered** — the speaker moves to the middle for everyone,
  themselves included.
- **Speaker centers with talking stick** — everyone else sees the speaker in the
  middle; the speaker sees the centre media instead of their own face.
- **Speaker stays in place** — the speaker's own circle grows where it is, and
  the centre keeps showing the scene.

If you are unsure, **centers with talking stick** is the gentlest for the person
speaking: they look at the room rather than at themselves.

## The goodbye URL

When you end a circle, everyone is redirected here — a feedback form, a follow-up
page, wherever makes sense. It must be a full `http(s)` address. Leave it blank
and people see the default goodbye screen instead.

Your admin may have set a site-wide default that is filled in for you; you can
replace it per room.

## Renaming a room

Renaming changes the URL, which invalidates the link everyone already has. Send
out the new one.

## Deleting a room

Deleting removes the room from the list. Historical usage records are kept, so
your reporting stays accurate.
