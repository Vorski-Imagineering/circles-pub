# Your host profile

Everything about you rather than about a room, in one place: **Profile** in the
sidebar.

## Photo

Shown beside your name in the admin area. Upload one, replace it, or delete it;
without one you get your initial.

This is your admin-area avatar. It is not what participants see in a circle —
there they see your live video, like everyone else.

## Name

First and last name. At least one is required — you cannot leave both blank. It
is how you are identified across the admin area, including on the room list as a
room's host.

## Host logo

Your own logo, shown in **all of your rooms**, replacing the site-wide logo.

The order is: a room-specific logo if one is set, otherwise your host logo,
otherwise the site logo. So this is how a facilitator running under their own
brand keeps that brand across every circle they hold, without setting it room by
room.

Upload, replace or delete it here. Deleting falls back to the site logo.

## Account

Your email address, shown for reference and not editable — it is the identity you
sign in with. Ask an admin if it needs to change.

Your role is shown here too: **Admin** or **Host**. Roles are set by admins; see
[People and access](../admin/people-and-access.md).
