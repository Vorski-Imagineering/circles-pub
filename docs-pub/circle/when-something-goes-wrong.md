# When something goes wrong

## The connection banner

If your connection to the circle is struggling, a banner appears telling you so,
and it tells you when things recover. It is about *your* connection, not anyone
else's — other people may be having a perfectly good time.

If the connection cannot be repaired on its own, the banner offers you a
reconnect. Nothing reconnects behind your back.

## Someone's video has frozen

If a person's picture stops moving and shows a spinner, their last frame is being
held while the connection to them is repaired. This is better than blanking them
out — you can still see who is there.

If it does not come back on its own, that person is usually best off reloading
their own page; there is nothing you can do from your side.

## Someone is missing

Remember that people appear only once their video does — see
[What you are looking at](the-circle.md). Someone who has just joined may take a
moment.

Someone who lost their camera entirely is removed from the circle and sent back
to the entry screen, so if a person vanishes, that is the likely reason.

## You have been returned to the entry screen

Two things do this:

- **Your camera became unavailable** — revoked, unplugged, or claimed by another
  app. The entry screen explains it and will not let you back in until a camera
  is available. See [Sound and camera](sound-and-camera.md).
- **You chose Settings from the menu**, which reloads the page.

In both cases the circle carries on without you and you can rejoin from the same
link.

## You cannot get in at all

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| An error about the room key | The link is wrong, incomplete, or no longer valid. Ask your host for a fresh one. |
| A login screen | The link had no key and you are not signed in as a host. |
| "We can't find a camera" | Another app has your camera, or none is attached. See [Joining a circle](joining-a-circle.md). |
| A message about the room being open elsewhere | The same browser already has this circle open in another tab. Close it. |

## The circle ended

If the host ends the circle, everyone leaves at once. You will either be sent to
a page the host chose, or shown a goodbye screen.
