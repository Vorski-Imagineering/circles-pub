# Reactions

## The heart

**Press and hold `L`.** A heart grows on your own circle, and everyone in the
room sees it. Let go and it fades away.

It is press-and-hold rather than a click: the heart grows for as long as you hold
the key, so a long press reads differently from a quick tap. There is no other
reaction and no counter — it is a way of showing warmth while someone else is
speaking, without interrupting them or unmuting.

## Where it appears

The heart appears **on your own circle**, not on the speaker's. Everyone can see
whose it is, which is the point: it is you visibly responding, not an anonymous
tally floating over the person talking.

The current speaker's circle does not show a heart.

## When it will not fire

- While you are typing in a text field, so `L` stays usable for typing.
- With `Cmd`, `Ctrl` or `Alt` held, so browser shortcuts like `Cmd+L` still work.

If you switch windows or tabs mid-press, the heart releases on its own rather
than staying stuck on your circle.
