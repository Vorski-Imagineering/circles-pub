# Branding and settings

**Settings** in the sidebar. Admin-only, and site-wide — every change here
affects every room and every host.

Changes save as you make them and reach everyone immediately, including people
currently in a circle. There is no draft state, so make wording changes when the
site is quiet.

## Logo

The site logo, shown on the entry screen participants see, and in the admin
sidebar.

It is the bottom of a chain of three. A room-specific logo wins if there is one;
otherwise the host's own logo (see
[Your host profile](../host/your-host-profile.md)); otherwise this. So this is
what people see when nobody has overridden it.

## Sponsor logo

A second logo shown alongside the first, for a funder or partner. Optional, and
independent of the chain above.

## Background and button colour

One colour, used for the entry screen background and the primary buttons across
the admin area. It is picked as a colour value, so you can match a brand exactly.

Choose something with enough contrast against white text — the colour is used
behind labels.

## Copy

| Field | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Welcome Title** | The heading on the entry screen. Replaces the room name when set. |
| **Welcome Text** | A short message under the heading. Supports line breaks. |
| **Admin Title Bar Text** | The name in the admin top bar. Useful for telling environments apart. |

Welcome title and text are the first thing a participant reads, before they have
entered anything. It is the right place to say what this space is, and — worth
saying — that their camera will be on.

## Default goodbye URL

Prefilled into new rooms as their goodbye destination. Hosts can override it per
room, and changing it here does not alter rooms that already exist.

Leave blank for no default.

## Allow self sign-up

Off by default: accounts are created only by invitation. Turning it on lets
people create their own host accounts unprompted.

This is the single widest-reaching switch on the page. Leave it off unless you
specifically want an open door — see [People and access](people-and-access.md).
