# People and access

**Users** in the sidebar. Admins only.

## Two roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Host** | Create and run their own rooms, browse the scene library read-only, see stats for their own rooms |
| **Admin** | Everything a host can, plus users, scenes, settings and site-wide usage |

There is no third role, and **participants are not users at all** — people
joining a circle need no account. Roles exist only for the admin area.

## Inviting someone

Choose to add a user, give their name and email, and pick the role. You get back
an invitation with a link, and a ready-written message you can copy and send.

The invite is what carries the access: they follow the link, sign in with that
email, and their account is created with the role you chose. Invitations expire,
so someone who waits too long needs a new one.

## Managing invitations

Pending invites are listed with their status. You can:

- **Regenerate** — issues a fresh link, for one that expired or went astray.
- **Delete** — withdraws the invitation entirely.

An invitation can only be accepted by the email address it was issued to.

## Signing in

Sign-in is by emailed link rather than a password — enter your email, receive a
link, follow it. There is nothing to remember and nothing to reset.

## Self sign-up

Off by default. When an admin turns it on in
[Branding and settings](branding-and-settings.md), people can create their own
accounts without an invitation. Leave it off unless you specifically want an
open door.

## Removing access

Delete the user. Their rooms are not deleted with them — reassign anything still
in use to another host first, or those rooms are left without an owner. See
[Rooms administration](rooms-administration.md).
