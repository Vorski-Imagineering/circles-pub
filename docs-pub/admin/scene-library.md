# Scene library

**Scenes** in the sidebar.

## What a scene is

A scene is the look of a circle: what sits behind everyone, and what sits in the
middle. Hosts choose a scene per room; they cannot create or change one.

A scene has:

| Part | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | No | Image or video filling the space behind the circles |
| **Centre** | No | Image or video in the middle, shown when nobody is speaking |
| **Talking stick image** | No | Shown to the speaker in the talking-stick layout, in place of their own face |
| **Centre video volume** | No | Whether centre video is audible, and how loud. Silent by default |
| **Background video volume** | No | Whether background video is audible, and how loud. Silent by default |
| **Category** | No | Which group it belongs to. See [Scene categories](scene-categories.md) |

Nothing in particular is required — a scene needs a name and **at least one** of
the parts above. A scene with no centre media simply leaves the middle empty
until someone speaks, and one with no background shows the plain backdrop.

## Media

Both background and centre take either an **image** (uploaded or by URL) or a
**video** (a YouTube link). One or the other, not both — choosing a video clears
the image and vice versa.

For videos, a thumbnail from the video is used wherever a still is needed, such
as the library tile and the room preview.

## Video volume

Both centre and background video are **silent by default**, which is almost
always right — a circle is people talking. Raise either only for a scene built
around ambient sound, and remember it plays for everyone.

## Creating one

**Add scene**, then:

1. Give it a name. Two scenes may share a name — they are told apart
   internally — so pick something you will recognise in the list.
2. Set at least one part — background, centre, talking stick or category.
   Until you set something, the scene cannot be created.

The preview beside the form shows the result as you go.

### From a room you have already set up

If a room already looks the way you want the scene to look, you do not have to
retype it. On the **Room Library**, each room card has a *Save this room's look
as a scene* button. It opens the Create Scene form filled in with what that room
actually shows — its own personalisation where it has some, its scene's values
where it does not — and you just give it a name.

The room itself is not touched: it keeps its own scene and its own overrides.
Nothing is saved until you press Create, so you can adjust anything first.

The **category is not copied** — pick one yourself, the same as for any other
new scene.

## Editing one

Open any scene from the library. Edits save as you make them, and **take effect
immediately in every room using that scene** — including circles happening right
now. Take care during hours when people are meeting.

## Finding scenes

The library has search and filters — by name, by what the centre holds, by what
the background holds, and by category. Filters are reflected in the address bar,
so a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared with another admin.

## Deleting one

Deleting removes a scene from future choices. Rooms already using it keep
working from their stored settings, and historical usage is preserved, but no
host can pick it again.

## Who can see this

Hosts can browse the library and start rooms from it, but cannot add, edit or
delete. Everything on this page is admin-only.
